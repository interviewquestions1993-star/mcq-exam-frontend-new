Editorial Policy

AI MCQ Trainer is an independent learning resource. This policy explains how we prepare static study guides and how to use automatically generated quiz questions responsibly.

1. Content Accuracy & Alignment

Our selected CBSE study guides explain common curriculum concepts in original wording. They are supplementary materials, not official NCERT or CBSE content, and do not replace the current syllabus, textbook, or instruction from a teacher.

2. AI-Assisted Content Generation

Interactive quizzes are generated automatically in response to the topic and settings selected by a visitor. Because each quiz is generated on demand, it is not individually reviewed before it is shown.

3. Human Oversight & Quality Control

Static guides and their worked questions are checked for clarity, internal consistency, and basic factual accuracy before publication. Automated quizzes can still contain factual, calculation, or explanation errors; important answers should be checked against authoritative sources. We do not claim a particular numerical accuracy rate or guarantee exam outcomes.

4. Corrections & Feedback

We welcome feedback from students and educators. If you spot an error in a guide or generated question, please include the page URL and a short description when contacting interviewquestions1993@gmail.com. We review submitted corrections and update material when appropriate.
